-Last-minute search for partners

FEAR OF FAILURE AND ITS CONSEQUENCES: 
1.	Fear of failure disables entrepreneurial learning or seeing failure as a learning opportunity. 
2.	Fear of failure impedes investment, opportunity recognition, and risk assessment. 
3.	Failure is often linked to grief and the emotional investment of entrepreneurs
4.	Often associated with collective norms that lead to internalizations that failure is negative 
5.	Crucial to entrepreneurial success is overcoming or managing a fear of failure and emotions linked to failure
