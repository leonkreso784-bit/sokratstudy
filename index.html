The four ways

Every subject — from the catalog or your own — opens in all four. Nothing to set up.

  • Learn

    The material itself — sections, images, tables, formulas.

  • Flashcards

    Question on one side, answer on the other. Short by design.

  • Quiz

    Multiple choice, scored, with the reason behind each answer.

  • Fill

    Type the missing term. Harder than recognising it in a list.

About Sokrat Study

Study what is ready, or make your own

Our Mission

Sokrat Study turns study material into something that asks you questions instead of just showing you answers. It is built by Leon Kreso and it is free — whether the material is already here or you write it yourself.

What We Do

Wherever the material comes from, it works the same way: read it, then practise it as flashcards, a quiz or fill-in-the-blanks. The catalogue holds ready university subjects; the editor lets you build your own from your notes.
