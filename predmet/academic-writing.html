What is a literature review?
A critical and evaluative description of publications on a chosen topic.
What is the difference between a 'thesis' and a 'dissertation' in the US system?
In the US, a dissertation is submitted for a PhD degree, while a thesis is submitted for a Master's degree.
What are the five means of scientific research?
Material, Informational, Mathematical, Logical, and Linguistic means.
What is a bibliographic database?
An organized collection of scientific and professional articles, book chapters, conference proceedings, and similar academic resources.
What are the two broad categories of publications based on durability of information?
Ephemeral and Enduring publications.
What are the two basic systems of bibliography presentation in Chicago style?
The Author-Date method and the Notes and Bibliography (Bibliographic) method.
How is a translation of a foreign-language book title added in Chicago Style?
In square brackets after the title and subtitle, without italics or quotation marks.
