Examples from the material

What is accounting?
Accounting is the process of recording, classifying, summarizing, and reporting an organization's financial transactions so users can make informed economic decisions.
What is a feasibility study?
An analysis performed BEFORE starting a business to determine whether the proposed venture is practical and likely to succeed — examining the market, location, costs, financing, and projected profitability.
What are the four basic financial statements?
1. Income Statement — revenues and expenses over a period 2. Statement of Owners' (Retained) Earnings — changes in equity 3. Balance Sheet — assets, liabilities, equity at a point in time 4. Statement of Cash Flows — cash inflows and…
What is a balance sheet?
A financial statement that lists a company's assets, liabilities, and owners' equity at a specific point in time, structured so that Assets = Liabilities + Owners' Equity.
What does the income statement measure?
It measures the results of operations over a period — total Revenues minus total Expenses = Net Income (or Net Loss).
What is double-entry bookkeeping?
A system in which every transaction is recorded with equal debits and credits in at least two accounts, keeping the accounting equation (A = L + E) in balance.
What is an accounting cycle?
The accounting cycle is the sequence of accounting procedures used to record, process, and report financial transactions during an accounting period, from initial transaction to preparation of financial statements and closing entries.
What is the USAR?
The Uniform System of Accounts for Restaurants — a standardized chart of accounts and reporting format so restaurants of all sizes can record and compare results consistently.
