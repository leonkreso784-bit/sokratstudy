What is the systems approach?
A view based on the idea that everything is inter-related and interdependent. A system is composed of related and dependent elements which, when in interaction, form a unitary whole — an assemblage or combination of parts forming a complex…
What is data?
Raw facts and figures that on their own have no meaning. Data can be any alphanumeric characters — text, numbers or symbols. Example: "15012002", "23", "English" mean nothing without context.
What is computer hardware?
The physical devices that make up a computer. A computer is a system composed of several components that all work together.
What is software?
A set of instructions, data, or programs used to operate a computer and execute specific tasks — software tells a computer how to function. It is a generic term for applications, scripts and programs.
What is a computer network?
Two or more computers connected so they can share their data, resources and applications.
What is the Internet?
An enormous network of networks — the massive computer network that connects devices using copper wires, wireless networks or fibre-optic cables.
What is e-business?

What is digital transformation?
A change in the way an organization operates — its systems, processes, workflow and culture. It brings data together across areas and uses automation and AI/ML to connect the customer journey.
