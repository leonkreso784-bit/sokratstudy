Examples from the material

What is the difference between service and hospitality?
Service is transactional, standardized, reactive and step-following; hospitality is personal, emotional, proactive and anticipates needs. Service is an expectation; hospitality is what makes a brand unforgettable.
What is the fundamental aim of economics as a science?
To investigate how to rationally use limited resources in order to satisfy largely unlimited needs in the best possible way; it studies how society produces and distributes goods and services.
How is a modern hospitality business conceptually defined?

In what forms can the assets of a hospitality company appear?
As money (or securities), as property (things), and as rights.

How is the business success of a hospitality company understood?
As a complex result of the interaction of internal and external factors. It is defined relative to the company's economic objectives (it is not just technical/architectural quality).
Why is price important in hospitality?
Price completes the hospitality product (without it the product is undefined), reflects its value, and is a constitutive element of the tourism market that directly affects demand.
