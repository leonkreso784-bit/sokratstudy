When and where did the term 'entrepreneur' originate?
1755 - From French word 'entreprendre' meaning (ability to take charge, to undertake) Introduced by Richard Cantillon who described entrepreneurs as economic agents who transform demand into supply.
What are the 4 key elements of psychological profiling of entrepreneurs?
1. NEED FOR ACHIEVEMENT - drive to excel 2. LOCUS OF CONTROL - belief in controlling outcomes 3. RISK-TAKING - willingness to take calculated risks 4. INNOVATION & CREATIVITY - generating new ideas

What is the difference between PLANNING and a PLAN?
PLANNING is a verb (implies action) - the process of envisioning the future. A PLAN is a written description of the future you envision for your business.
What are the 2 main categories of reasons why firms fail?
1. EXOGENOUS FACTORS - external forces, 'survival of the fittest', hostile jolts 2. INTERNAL FACTORS - leadership, management, poor decision-making, strategic errors
