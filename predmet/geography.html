Examples from the material

How is the first midterm structured?
10 questions total: 5 closed-type + 5 open-type short answers.
What is the origin and meaning of the word "geography"?
From Ancient Greek γεωγραφία (geōgraphía) = "earth description". Geography studies Earth's features and the relationships between people and their environments.
Give 2 valid county centres that often appear in map drills.
Examples: Šibenik, Sisak, Rijeka, Zadar, Dubrovnik.
What mountain system shapes Croatian relief, and how was it formed?
The Dinarides — part of the Alpide belt — formed by tectonic plates colliding during the Alpine orogeny, which began in the Cretaceous period.
What are the three Croatian tourist macro-regions?
Coastal, Mountainous, and Pannonian tourist region.
What visual cue strongly identifies Dubrovnik?
Walled Old Town on a rocky peninsula with distinct fortifications.
What does the second part of the course (Tourism Geography of the World) cover?
The tourism geography of the world's continents and regions: Europe, Asia, Africa, Australia & Oceania, and the Americas.
What is Europe's area and its role in world history?
About 10,368,047 km²; the centre of the "old world", the origin of colonization and modern global civilization, with a rich cultural/historical heritage and high well-being.
