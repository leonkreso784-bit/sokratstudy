What does macroeconomics study, and how does it differ from microeconomics?
Macroeconomics studies the functioning of the economy as a WHOLE — overall national production, employment, prices and foreign trade ("macros" = big in Greek). Microeconomics analyses individual prices, quantities and markets of…
How is the labour force defined, and who is excluded?
The labour force consists of the employed and the unemployed: \( L = E + U \) Excluded (NOT in the labour force): students, homemakers, retirees, those unable to work, and those who do not want to work.
What is GDP?
Gross Domestic Product is the market value of all FINAL products and services produced in a country during one year — the most comprehensive measure of a country’s total economic production.
What is the fundamental national-accounting identity?
Total production = Total consumption = Total income. Economic activity can be measured three equivalent ways, all giving the SAME value.
What is the consumption function?
\( C = c_0 + c_1 Y_D \) • \( c_0 \) = autonomous consumption (consumption when disposable income is zero) • \( c_1 \) = the marginal propensity to consume (MPC), with \( 0 < c_1 < 1 \) • \( Y_D \) = disposable income
What is money in this model?
Money = currency (cash) + checkable deposits — the financial asset that can be used directly to buy goods. The alternative is bonds, which pay interest but cannot be spent directly.
