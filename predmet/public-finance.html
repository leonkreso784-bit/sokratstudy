Examples from the material

Public finance (definition)
The field of economics that analyses government taxation and spending policies and their influence on resource allocation and income distribution.
Public goods (slide description)
The general welfare of the people, the best interests of the community.
Externality
A cost or benefit that occurs when the activity of one entity directly affects the welfare of another in a way that is outside the market mechanism.
Why have social security? (main reason)
Consumption smoothing and insurance against living longer than expected and using up retirement assets too early; also covers disabled, dependents and survivors.
Who can bear taxes?
Only people (owners, workers, customers), not firms or things.
Excess burden
A cost of a tax beyond the tax revenue collected: a loss of welfare above and beyond taxes collected.
