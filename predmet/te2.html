Examples from the material

What is the origin of the word 'tour'?
From the Latin word 'tornus', meaning 'a tool for making a circle'.
What is tourism demand?
The WILLINGNESS and ABILITY of consumers to buy different amounts of a tourism product at different prices during any period of time.
Why is forecasting tourism demand important?
In a changing global environment, reliable short-term and long-term forecasts of tourism activity are essential for both government policy development and business planning.
What are the 3 basic forms of inputs (factors of production) into tourism supply?
Natural resources, human resources, and human-made resources.
What is market structure and what does it affect?
Market structure refers to the NATURE of the market within which a tourism firm operates. It affects the firm's CONDUCT (decision-making processes) and the firm's PERFORMANCE (potential to make profit, gain market share and achieve…
How important is price as a decision variable for a tourism firm?
Price is not the ONLY decision variable, but it is perhaps the MOST CRITICAL — and it is the MOST ADJUSTABLE. Unlike product, distribution and promotion (which take time to change), price can be altered QUICKLY.
Where does the economic contribution of tourism start, and what is the basic measure?
It starts with TOURIST EXPENDITURE. The basic measure is expenditure injected by type of visitor, by visitor night, and by length of stay.
What is tourism expenditure?
The total consumption expenditure made by a visitor, or on behalf of a visitor, for goods and services during their trip and stay at the destination — including payments made in advance or after the trip for services received during it.
