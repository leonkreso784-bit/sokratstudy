Direct Expenditure - suppliers who sell goods and services directly to tourists.
Indirect Expenditure - when firms receiving tourist expenditure purchase inputs from other firms
Induced Expenditure  (managers, workers and their families)- spend their incomes “downstream”- (supermarkets, bookstores, ..)
Multiplier = Total Impact / Direct Expenditure
The total effect is the sum of the direct, indirect, and induced effects 
total effects increases in ➢ output ➢ sales ➢ value added ➢ GDP ➢ household income ➢employment
five types of multipliers- sales multiplier, output multiplier, income multiplier, value-added multiplier, employment multiplier.

TSA – tourism satellite accounts - a standard statistical framework and the main tool for the economic measurement of tourism.
Key measures in TSA- Tourism expenditure, Tourism consumption, Tourism output, Tourism gross added value – TGVA , Tourism gross domestic product – TGDP, Tourism gross operating surplus – TOGP, Tourism employment, Tourism exports, Tourism imports.
